🥽 VR Controller Instructions

Setup: Open https://loading... in your headset browser and tap Enter VR.
Activate arm: Hold Grip (Squeeze) on a controller to enable position/rotation tracking for that arm.
Gripper: Pull Trigger to close; release to open.
Chest yaw: Frozen by default. Press B on the right controller to toggle freeze; while unfrozen, the chest-yaw reference tracks your headset's facing direction.
Reference frames: Click left thumbstick to toggle the rb + chest reference axes in VR.
